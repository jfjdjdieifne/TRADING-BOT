Q3  WHY THE 4H TRAIL IS DEAD -- AND WHAT 1H/30m OFFERED INSTEAD
==========================================================================
  3.0  THE CODE (strategy_v36_ict_champion.py, _manage):
       `TRAIL_4H_ONLY_AFTER_3R` DOES NOT EXIST in the engine --
       grep finds it nowhere. The real switch is
       TRAIL_4H_ONLY_AFTER_LOCK = True, and when it is on the
       post-lock trail searches `srcs = [self.struct_canvas]`
       ONLY. struct_canvas is STRUCT_TF = '4h'.
       So after the +3R lock the engine is blind to 1H/30m/15m
       swing lows by construction, and to the 4H lens itself
       unless that 4H canvas has confirmed a NEW BlueBox_SSL
       above floor_line but below price, AFTER the entry.

  the four conditions a 4H box must satisfy to move the stop:
    (i)   state == ACTIVE          (ii) above floor_line
    (iii) strictly below current price
    (iv)  confirmed AFTER the entry (start_idx > entry_idx)
  A 4H box needs 12 HOURS to confirm a 3-bar fractal. Most
  trades are dead before the next 4H box exists at all.

  3.1  THE BREACH -- trades that DID reach the +3R lock
