1. > An ICT Fair Value Gap (FVG) is a three-candle price imbalance pattern in the Inner Circle Trader methodology where price moves aggressively in one direction
2. > The middle candle expands so quickly that buyers and sellers do not transact evenly, creating a liquidity imbalance in the market structure.
