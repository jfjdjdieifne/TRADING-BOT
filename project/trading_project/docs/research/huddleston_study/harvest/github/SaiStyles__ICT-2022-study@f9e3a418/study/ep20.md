# Ep20 - The London setup he called the night before

**In one line:** He calls a DXY level *in advance* on ep19, it hits, and he uses it to teach the **inverse-pairing method** for forex - plus the sharpest statement yet of what disqualifies a fair value gap.

## The spine

1. **A forward call, checkable against the record.** [02:41] `go to episode 19 - the one I did last night - and listen to the **20 minute mark, around 10 seconds** ... you'll hear me outline the very thing you're seeing in your chart right now`. The level: **99.92** on the dollar index [09:57]. [02:30] `I can't edit a video on YouTube - once it's there, it's there`.

2. **How the level was derived.** [04:27] Simply: the run-up at the start of a prior candle, `just draw that out in time` [04:38], giving a **3-pip window, 99.95-99.92** [04:41]. [04:49] `that's what I was eyeballing last night`. He notes he has finer tools he is choosing not to teach [04:56].

3. **THE DISPLACEMENT DISQUALIFIER - the clearest statement in the corpus.** [12:39] Asked why he used one low and not another:
   > `the fair value gap forms right here on this candle. once that candle closes, the very next candle opens essentially **right at that low** - so **it hasn't really moved away**, and hasn't really shown me displacement. **something energetic where it moves away from that fair value gap - that's the important factor.**`

   Restated as an entry rule [13:53]: `it's not going to be placed in the marketplace for a shorting opportunity or entry on limit anytime in here **because it has to move away from the fair value gap**`.

4. **THE INVERSE PAIRING METHOD.** [09:16] `you have to use **intermarket relationships**`. [09:27] `if you're looking at euro, that's your trading market - you have to have a **barometer**, something to measure its movement with`.

   The logic [09:49]: dollar reaching a bullish level -> euro rallying into it is **the false breakout**. [10:33] `the moment it hits this level, it's the same moment the dollar index is hitting that area I was outlining for a buying opportunity`. [10:46] `**this is a false breakout because my analysis was done and shared with you openly**`.

5. **Do not chase; the killzone is still open.** [14:15] `it doesn't get to it here, it goes a little bit deeper. **we wait. we don't chase it.** we don't go in here thinking oh well, I missed the move - we still have time, the killzone for London open is still open.`

6. **He did not take the trade, and does not mind.** [11:03] `could I have bought that? sure ... did I? no - **I went to sleep, I was tired**`. [11:15] `I don't want to be in every single move`.

7. **1% is the maximum, and less is better.** [15:41] `think about it in terms of **fixed percentage risk on equity** ... take how much money you have in the account times **one percent. one percent is the maximum** - preferably you should be doing a half of one percent, or maybe a quarter of one percent.`

## Contradicts / refines

- **Sharpens displacement into a testable condition.** Ep05 gave the elephant analogy; ep06 added "must close beyond"; ep20 adds the decisive one: **the next candle must OPEN AWAY from the gap, not at it** [12:39]. That is fully codeable and it is the first criterion that disqualifies a gap on *structure* rather than on feel.
- **Confirms ep07's intermarket method and inverts its role.** Ep07 used ES as an entry *trigger* for NQ. Here DXY is a **barometer** that identifies a *false breakout* in EUR [10:46] - i.e. a filter, not a signal. Consistent with the ep07 warning that correlation confirms rather than generates.
- **Tightens the risk numbers again.** Ep07 [04:52] said students should risk under 1%. Ep20 [15:47] makes 1% the stated **maximum** with 0.25-0.5% preferred. His own 3.5-4.5% (ep03, ep07) remains a separate, personal figure.
- **He explicitly walls this off from the model.** [03:11] `this is **not** the model for my daughter, this is **not** an addition to the model - I'm just answering questions`. **Do not fold the London/forex material into the core spec on the strength of this episode.**

## Reader's note

**[12:39] is the most useful line in the episode and probably the most useful displacement criterion in the corpus.**

Every prior definition of displacement was qualitative - energetic, animated, not lethargic, closes beyond. This adds a structural test that requires no threshold at all: **after the gap-forming candle closes, does the next candle open away from the gap, or at it?** If it opens at the low, there was no displacement and the gap does not qualify. That single check would have excluded the rejected setups in ep04 and ep05 without needing to invent a magnitude.

Two cautions:

1. **He withholds the tools that produced the precision** [05:11] - the level was `eyeballed` from a candle extension, and he says his real method fine-tunes to 2-3 pips but is reserved for his private group. **The 99.92 call is therefore not fully reproducible from this corpus**, and should not be cited as evidence the taught method achieves that precision.
2. **He frames this as a demonstration, not doctrine** [03:11]. The London session, DXY pairing and forex application are answers to viewer questions - explicitly outside the model he is building. Keep them in the corpus (section 12 filters nothing) but flag them as **out-of-model** when the spec is assembled.
