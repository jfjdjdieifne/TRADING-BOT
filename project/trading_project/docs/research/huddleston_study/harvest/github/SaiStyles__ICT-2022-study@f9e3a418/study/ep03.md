# Ep03 - "Internal Range Liquidity and Market Structure Shift"

**In one line:** He defines the **order block** properly for the first time - and it is not what the internet says it is - then gives the session times and takes a live NQ trade for +$1,190 narrating every decision.

## The spine

1. **"Shift", not "break" - and the distinction is deliberate.** [02:21] `I see a lot of people try to teach my concepts ... and we'll use that term interchangeably` - he will not. [02:47] A **shift** is intraday and `[03:22] just means that there's likely a downside draw or an upside draw intraday`. A **break** `[03:17] means a little bit more in context`, i.e. prolonged multi-day movement. [02:55] An intraday bearish break `may just lead to an intraday price leg that may eventually see that high be taken out in the same day`.

2. **A structure shift is meaningless without a liquidity run first.** [07:05] `that's significant only - ONLY - if this run down here has traded into sell stops`. [10:10] Repeated: `that is significant only on the basis that we have taken liquidity out of the marketplace`. **He says "only" three times in five minutes.** This is the single most emphasised conditional in the episode.

3. **No candle close is required.** [13:47] `high is taken here, we traded above it - it does not need to close above that. Okay, real important.` A wick through is enough.

4. **THE ORDER BLOCK, defined.** [24:28] `it's just change in the state of delivery`. Concretely: a run of same-direction candles is delivering price one way; find the **opening price of the candle that began that run**; when price violates that opening, the state of delivery has changed and that opening becomes the reference level. [24:58] `that's why it's sensitive - the algorithm remembers that right there`.

5. **Not every candle is an order block.** [29:36] `you think every down closed candle is a bullish order block and up [close] that one is a bearish order block - and that's not the case`. A high-probability one needs **three** things at once (below).

6. **Sessions and hours are specific.** Three session windows, a hard stop at noon, and a preferred window. Table below.

7. **Internal range liquidity, defined.** [35:02] `looking for short-term lows or short-term highs inside a price leg that we're retracing back into ... a short term high or low with stops above or below it, or an imbalance in that same range`.

## Procedure taught

### The high-probability order block - three conditions, all required

[23:55] `why is this a good bearish order block? because it has that gap, and it's taken liquidity, and there's a market structure shift.`

| # | condition | at |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a **fair value gap** is present | [23:57] |
| 2 | **liquidity has been taken** (stops run) | [24:00] |
| 3 | a **market structure shift** has occurred | [24:03] |

Then: [25:23] `inside this fair value gap, this opening price on the order block - that's your buy, plus three pips or whatever for spread, and that's what you would use for a limit order.`

## Contradicts / refines

- **Refines ep02's "break in market structure":** ep02 used *break* throughout [28:00]. Here he splits the term - intraday is a **shift**, multi-day is a **break** - and says the interchange is other people's error. **When reading ep02's step 4, read "shift".**
- **Refines ep02's entry:** ep02 entered anywhere in the gap [29:21]. Here it is sharpened to the **order block's opening price inside the gap**, as a limit order [25:23].
- **Extends ep02's session note:** ep02 gave 8:30-11:00 [42:40]. Here that is extended to noon with a hard prohibition after it [34:01].

## Reader's note

Three things a backtester must get right from this episode:

1. **The order block is a PRICE (an opening), not a zone or a candle body.** [24:36] It is the open of the candle that started the delivery run. Most public interpretations use the candle's body or full range - he explicitly says that is wrong [29:36].
2. **The three conditions are simultaneous, not alternatives.** FVG **and** liquidity taken **and** structure shift [23:55]. Any two is not the setup.
3. **The stacked-FVG rule at [42:09] is a real, codeable entry filter** - and its veto clause [43:29] is one of the only "do not take the trade" conditions he states anywhere. Both are easy to miss because they are narrated live rather than taught as a rule.
