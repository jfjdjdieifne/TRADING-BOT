# Ep24 - The setup that didn't form, and why that proves the model

**In one line:** He publicly flagged a level to watch, the setup **failed to appear**, no trade was taken - and he argues that this is the strongest evidence the model works.

## The spine

1. **THE NON-SETUP AS VALIDATION - the key idea.** [17:22]
   > `this is actually a good lesson because it shows where I'm looking for something to form, **but it doesn't form**. it doesn't create a losing trade, it doesn't get me into something that causes me to buy when there really isn't an entry pattern there. **this is the proof, the evidence that the model has validity.**`

   [17:44] `if you have valid, reasonable, sound logic behind the model ... **it should prevent you from taking bad trades**`.

2. **The criteria, checked and failed - live.** [22:37] Price traded down into the marked fair value gap. Then:
   - [22:46] Initial criterion met: `trading below an old low` -> sell stops taken ✓
   - [23:01] Next requirement: `it's got to trade back above this old low - it's got to have **displacement** above that`
   - [23:12] **`did it happen here? no.`**
   - [23:27] `it just went down, consolidated, returned back to it, but **melted** - didn't do anything above that old low. **there was no displacement, no energetic move**`

   [23:42] Therefore: `anyone that would have looked at this and bought today **is not using what I'm teaching** - they're just impulsively pushing a button`.

3. **WHERE SETUPS FORM - the full sequence, stated compactly.** [32:23]
   > `run above an old high or relative equal highs. if you're bearish that sets up a scenario where the market goes up, **sweeps** the highs, breaks back down below the highs, **short-term lows taken out with displacement**, and then **you go back through that displacement leg looking for a fair value gap to short into**`

   Note he uses **sweeps** precisely, per ep23's distinction.

4. **THE TRIGGER: the nearest short-term low.** [32:53] Drawn as a diagram:
   > `price is moving towards [the relative equal highs], small correction, it runs towards it again, and then finally **it runs through** those relative equal highs. at that moment, as soon as it goes above it, **you start panning back through all this price action and find the nearest short-term low - because that right there is going to be your trigger**`

5. **Price marks WHERE, time marks WHEN.** [21:52] The fair value gap `by itself is just price - but we have to incorporate what element first? **algorithmically, it's time.**` [22:08] `so I'm teaching you elements of time at that 9:30`.

6. **What a flagged level actually means.** [21:24] `this here is just **where the trade might form**. hear what I just said - where the trade **might**. it could. it may not.` [21:38] `it's absolutely going to - this is a promise, there's no way this can fail - **that logic doesn't exist in trading**`.

7. **Accountability, at length.** [15:46] `take accountability for your actions`. [16:07] `I didn't tell anybody about a trade - **I said study. watch. I'm watching this.**` [28:55] `**never, ever champion me for your success** ... you can thank me for helping you with the logic, but I didn't help you make money - and just as well, I did not help you lose it either`.

8. **Endure losses - a journal exercise.** [30:53]
   > `in your journal right now, with today's date, make a promise to yourself. write it out: **I promise that as long as I'm doing this, I'm going to endure losses.** notice I said **endure** - you're not going to be defeated by those losses.`

   [31:14] `trading is all about **managing losing trades**`. [31:30] `**every trader is a losing trader that finds success out of loss, or suffers ruin in it.** that's it - that's the only two things that can happen.`

## Contradicts / refines

- **Supplies the trigger-selection rule that ep02-23 left implicit.** Which short-term low? [32:53] answers it: **the nearest one to the left, identified the moment the highs are run.** Every prior episode says "a short-term low" without saying which. This is directly codeable and removes a real ambiguity.
- **Confirms ep20's displacement disqualifier from the opposite direction.** Ep20 [12:39] said the gap fails if the next candle opens *at* it. Ep24 [23:27] adds the level-based version: after sweeping a level, price must **displace back through it** - `melting` back is not displacement. **Two independent disqualifiers now exist; both are structural, neither needs a magnitude threshold.**
- **Reinforces the do-nothing thread** (ep19, ep21) with the strongest framing yet: the absence of a trade is *evidence for* the model [17:41], not a failure of it.
- **Restates ep22's point that the entry is the easy part** - here as `price marks where, time marks when` [21:52].

## Reader's note

**[17:22] is worth carrying into the model spec explicitly, because it changes how a backtest should be evaluated.**

His claim is that a valid model earns its keep partly through **trades it prevents**. That is measurable: a backtest should record not only the trades taken but the **candidate setups rejected at each gate** - swept level with no displacement, displacement with no gap, gap outside the displacement range. If the rejected set contains a high proportion of moves that would have lost, the model's filters are doing real work. If the rejected set performs the same as the accepted set, the filters are decoration.

**That is a genuinely testable proposition and nothing in the corpus so far invites it as directly as this episode does.**

The other durable item is the trigger rule at [32:53] - *the nearest short-term low, found the moment the highs are run*. It converts "find a swing low" from a judgement into a lookup, and combined with ep11's nesting (which swing high matters) and ep24's rule (which swing low matters), the structural selection problem is now essentially solved from the corpus itself.
