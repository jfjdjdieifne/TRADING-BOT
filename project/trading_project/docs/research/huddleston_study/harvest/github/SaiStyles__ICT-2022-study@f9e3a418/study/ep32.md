# Ep32 - Consolidation days, and how to see one coming

**In one line:** A day-type classifier: **an outside day that closes down, followed by a failure to reach the old low, predicts chop** - and on such days the only real setup comes in the **3:00-4:00 PM** window.

## The spine

1. **OUTSIDE DAY, defined and used as a predictor.** [01:46]
   > `an outside day is a day that's **higher than the previous day and lower than the previous day's low**`

   [01:36] What follows it: `when this happens there's typically this type of price action formation ... if you're trading down to an old low **but fall short of it** - this is usually where that double bottom idea comes in and it feels safe ... **but if they stop it short, it tends to be a choppy day** - especially if we have an outside day with a down close.`

   **Observed:** the old daily low was **3855**; price reached **3856** [02:29] - `we went one handle short of that`, then chopped all day.

2. **CONSOLIDATION DAY, defined.** [11:17]
   > `the algorithm on days like this - **this is called a consolidation day** - the market starts trading, **creates an initial range, and then stays in that range until the afternoon at three o'clock**`

3. **THE 3:00-4:00 PM WINDOW, with its origin.** [11:32]
   > `**it used to be the bond close** that started this whole thing, but now it's just three o'clock to four o'clock, when equities close - the closing bell - **that sets the tone for on-close orders**`

   He called the window publicly in advance [06:30]: `take a vertical line and add it to **three o'clock** New York local time, and **four o'clock** - and that would be the setup that forms`.

4. **THE MIDDLE OF THE RANGE IS 50/50 - avoid it.** [04:40]
   > `I mentioned how it was likely to be a **50-50 probability** ... it really **statistically moves to 50-50 when we start trading back into the middle of the range**`

   And on a consolidation day the middle becomes the **target** rather than a place to trade from [06:57]: `the main thing was focusing on the middle of the range`.

5. **The consolidation-day setup, stated as a sequence.** [07:22]
   > `if we're looking for setups based on a **run above for buy side and pull back into the range**, it needs to be specific in terms of time - I gave a very specific window between three o'clock and four o'clock`

   Why the run happens [07:23]: to squeeze shorts who trailed stops down, **and** to induce longs so that sell-side liquidity builds beneath them.

6. **What the model rejected.** [04:26] Earlier in the day, a fair value gap that price bumped and traded lower from: `nothing really in here to work with in terms of the model, **so nothing here is a short**`. Third consecutive episode (ep24, ep30, ep32) documenting explicit rejections.

## Contradicts / refines

- **Adds a day-type classifier the corpus lacked.** Ep19 and ep21 taught recognising a *heavy* day; ep32 [01:36] gives the signature of a **choppy** one, in advance, from two observable facts: outside day + down close, then failure to reach the old low. **Codeable from daily bars alone.**
- **Explains the 3:00 PM spool from ep30.** Ep30 [05:11] observed the 3:00 PM move without a reason. Ep32 [11:32] supplies it: on-close orders, historically anchored to the bond close. **Same phenomenon, now with a mechanism.**
- **Adds "avoid the middle" as an explicit filter.** Ep02/ep16 used the 50% for premium/discount; ep27 used it as an entry gate. Ep32 [04:56] states the consequence directly - **mid-range is where edge decays to nothing.** That is a useful negative rule and it is consistent with the premium/discount logic rather than additional to it.
- **Third documented rejection in three episodes** (ep24, ep30, ep32). The pattern is now firmly established and worth carrying into the spec as a testable expectation.

## Reader's note

**Two directly implementable items.**

1. **The chop predictor** [01:36]. Yesterday outside + down close, today falls short of the old low -> expect consolidation, stand down until 3:00 PM. Every input is a daily bar comparison. This is the first *forward-looking* day-type call in the corpus - ep19 and ep21 classified the day from intraday behaviour, this one classifies it before the session gets going.

2. **The 3:00-4:00 PM window** [11:32] as the consolidation-day setup slot. It composes with the rest of the intraday clock now assembled across the corpus: 8:30 (embargo/open), 9:30 (equities open, Judas), 12:00-1:00 (lunch, no entries, liquidity built), 1:30 (PM re-entry), 3:00-4:00 (on-close), 3:40/3:50/4:00 (MOC acceleration).

**One caution on evidence:** his son's live account growing $6,000 to $10,000 in a week [13:11] is presented as corroboration. It is a single week, one account, unaudited, and by the corpus's own standards (ep18's demonstration trades, ep15's no-stop entries) results of this kind are context, not proof. Record it; do not weight it.
