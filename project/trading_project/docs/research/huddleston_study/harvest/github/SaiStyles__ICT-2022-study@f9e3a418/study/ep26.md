# Ep26 - "Example of Tape Reading Practice" - trading AGAINST his own daily bias

**In one line:** He deliberately takes a long **against** his bearish daily bias, narrates it live, pyramids into six contracts, and explains exactly why the stop sits where it does.

## The spine

1. **He states the violation up front.** [00:11] `**I'm gonna be trading against daily bias** and going long here`.

   The justification [04:19]:
   > `even though I'm trading against the daily bias - the fact that we traded down **three times** intraday and took out yesterday's low, and by doing so, **that lack of follow-through** kind of bolsters [the case]`

   **Repeated failure to follow through after taking sell-side is what licenses the counter-trend trade.**

2. **The premise: sell side already taken.** [02:02] `this here is the sell side liquidity - the market has dropped below those relative equal lows. **I bought those sell stops.**` [03:26] Price dipped into a fair value gap then ran the relative equal lows [03:32], so [03:40] `I'm looking for expansion to the upside`.

3. **He is explicitly fading the crowd that now follows imbalances.** [10:00]
   > `traders are now trying to go short because they looked at that imbalance, the 8:30, the quick sudden drop - the market has come back and rebalanced that, now it's starting to drift lower. **people that follow imbalances ... because they've gotten real popular lately** - that idea, they're trying to sell short on. **I'm going against that and I'm targeting them.** so that's a buy right there.`

   Also [03:43]: `retail sees that as a bear flag - they're thinking lower prices. I'm not.`

4. **STOP PLACEMENT, with the reasoning stated.** [05:00]
   > `why do I feel confident placing my stop loss there? because if you look where I have my stop, it's basically inside of **the lower end of that fair value gap and the old short-term high around 9:30**. so I have **market structure supporting the idea**, and we've already taken sell side liquidity out - we're back inside the range.`

   [04:52] The test: `it should not trade back down into that. **if it does, I'm wrong, and that's fine.**`

5. **Pyramiding, and NOT rushing the stop.** [04:33] `I'm gonna do two more` -> [04:47] `now I have six contracts long, and I'll move my stop up just a little bit`. Later [10:55] `notice **I'm not in a hurry to rush my stop-loss up**`. Only near target [12:43] does he move to breakeven-plus.

6. **Scale-out plan set in advance.** [05:26] `buy side liquidity is the next draw - **I'll take four of the contracts off**`. [12:12] Executed on a **market order** above the buy-side high. The final two were stopped out on the trailed stop [13:59] - he reports that plainly.

7. **Watch price, not P&L - again.** [03:15] `try not to worry about that profit and loss, that's the distraction. **pay attention to the price.**`

## Procedure taught - the counter-bias long

| # | step | at |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily bias is **bearish** - this trade is against it | [00:11] |
| 2 | Price takes **sell side** below relative equal lows (3 attempts, no follow-through) | [02:08], [04:22] |
| 3 | Price is inside a fair value gap (~4000 level) | [00:26] |

| 8 | Take 4 of 6 off above buy-side liquidity, market order | [05:26], [12:12] |
| 9 | Trail the last two; stopped out | [13:59] |

## Contradicts / refines

- **He trades against his own bias, which ep18 said bias exists to prevent.** Ep18 [12:12] framed bias as a filter that excludes half the market. Here he takes the excluded side [00:11]. **The reconciling condition is his own:** three failed attempts at sell-side follow-through [04:22]. That is a stated, checkable licence - but it is a genuine exception to the ep18 rule and should be logged as such.
- **Gives the clearest stop-placement *reasoning* in the corpus.** Ep06/ep09/ep12/ep22 all say *where* to put the stop. [05:00] says *why*: it must sit where the structure supporting the idea would be broken - inside the gap's far edge and beyond the relevant short-term high. **That is the generalisable principle behind all the specific stop rules.**
- **Confirms ep21's "no follow-through = information" reading** from the other direction. Ep21: no rally on a bearish day = very heavy. Ep26: **no continuation after taking sell-side = the sellers have failed**, so fade them.
- **First explicit statement that his concepts have become crowded** [10:00]. He is now fading traders using the imbalance idea he popularised. **This has a real consequence for backtesting: the edge he describes may behave differently after 2022 as the pattern became widely known.** Worth flagging in the hypothesis ledger.

## Reader's note

**Two durable items from a short episode.**

1. **The counter-bias licence** [04:22]: repeated failure to follow through after liquidity is taken. Stated as a count - *three* attempts at yesterday's low with no continuation. That is codeable and it is the only condition anywhere in the corpus that permits trading against the daily bias.

2. **The stop-placement principle** [05:00]: the stop belongs at the price where **the structure that justified the trade** would be invalidated - not at a fixed distance, and not merely "below the low". Every earlier stop rule (ep06's candle 1/2, ep12's swing high, ep22's gap-creating candle) is an instance of this one idea.

And note the crowding admission at [10:00]. He is explicitly targeting traders acting on the fair-value-gap concept he taught. **A model whose author says he profits from other people trading it is a model whose historical edge should not be assumed to persist unchanged** - that belongs in the ledger alongside the hypothesis count, not buried in a page.
