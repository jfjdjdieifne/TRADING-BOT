# Ep36 - "No model entry here - that's telling"

**In one line:** The absence of a setup is read as *information about intent*, plus a personal risk rule that explains a lot of his behaviour: **he tries to be done trading by Wednesday.**

## The spine

1. **NO SETUP = THE MARKET TIPPING ITS HAND.** [05:57]
   > `notice **there's no model entry here. that's telling. okay, it's tipping its hand to you** - because **there's no real setup and it's a rush to get down here.** it's clearing out sell side liquidity going down, rallying, suckering in longs trying to pick the bottom, knocking those individuals out.`

   [06:16] `now they are **not allowed** to be long - their stops taken. **nobody in retail usually will take a re-entry after something like that** - they're afraid.`

   **This is the third distinct use of "no setup" in the corpus**: ep24 (proof the model filters), ep30 (a sell-off with no setup), and here - **the absence itself indicates urgency and therefore direction.**

2. **DONE BY WEDNESDAY.** [08:04]
   > `**I typically like to have all my trading done by Wednesday.** and because I've done what I've shown here today, **I'm satisfied with this particular week.**`

   [08:16] Pre-empting the objection: `some of you are going to be like, this is crazy - you have Wednesday still, you have Thursday, Friday. you're welcome to do that, but **I have to be disciplined**. being consistent - how you get there is **having rules, rules of engagement**.`

   And the reason to stop [08:38]: `**it can go either direction** ... because I don't have a clear definitive objective, **I'm going to sit on my hands**`.

3. **WHICH structure shift is the right one.** [05:19] Anticipating the confusion directly:
   > `we had a short-term little shift in market structure here, and I know some of you see this and you see this one here - **which one's the shift in market structure?** well, **I'm playing the run up here to a premium** ... **so if this is a range I'm working within**, the shift in market structure occurs here when it takes out that high.`

   **The rule: the relevant structure shift is the one inside the range you have defined.** Define the range first, then the shift is unambiguous.

4. **A reason to expect the retracement to hold.** [05:45] `**I didn't believe we were going to take out the lows here, because we should have done it at 8:30 news** - it didn't do it. so this is a deep retracement, and then sent it higher into a premium right before 9:30.`

   **If the news window did not produce the move, expect it not to come.**

5. **Oversold without an indicator.** [01:14] Price below equilibrium and inside a fair value gap: `we have a market that is in what - **an oversold condition. do we need an indicator to plot that? nope, absolutely not.**` [01:24] The rectangle and Fib exist only `to draw special attention for you, the viewer`.

6. **Contract rollover, answering a viewer question.** [00:22] `these contracts are delivered by contract month ... we're about to end trading of the June contract, then we'll roll over into September 2022`.

## Contradicts / refines

- **Third and strongest form of the "no setup" argument.** Ep24 [17:41]: absence proves the model filters. Ep30 [08:03]: a real sell-off produced nothing. Ep36 [05:57]: **the absence itself is directional information** - a move without a setup is a move in a hurry, and that urgency tells you what it is doing (clearing liquidity) and therefore what comes next. **This is the most sophisticated use of a rejection in the corpus.**
- **Answers "which structure shift?" - a genuine ambiguity across ep02-35.** [05:19] The answer is procedural: **define your dealing range first**, and the shift is the one that breaks inside it. Every earlier episode assumed the reader would pick the right one.
- **Adds a weekly stopping rule to the risk framework.** The corpus already had: max 4 trades/day (ep16), one loss then stop (ep15), monthly target then stop (ep13, ep15), skip the day after an overnight run (ep09). **Done by Wednesday** [08:04] is the weekly-scale version, and the stated reason is the absence of a clear objective, not a profit target.
- **Adds a news-window inference.** [05:45] If 8:30 did not produce the expected move, do not expect it later. **Consistent with ep17's treatment of news as a timing device rather than an information source.**

## Reader's note

**[05:57] deserves attention because it inverts the usual logic.**

Everywhere else, no setup means stand aside. Here, no setup on a fast move is read as evidence that the move is **liquidity clearance rather than delivery** - and therefore that the real move is still ahead, in the other direction. That is a genuinely different inference and it is testable: **on moves that lack a qualifying setup, does price reverse more often than on moves that contain one?** If yes, the absence carries information exactly as he claims.

The **done-by-Wednesday** rule [08:04] is worth recording in the model spec even though it is personal discipline rather than mechanics. Combined with the other stopping rules, the pattern across the corpus is unmistakable: **his constraint is almost never finding trades - it is refusing them.** A backtest that trades every qualifying setup Monday to Friday is not modelling how he operates, and will show both more trades and different risk characteristics than his actual record.
