# Ep01 - The contract he is making with you

**In one line:** Not a lesson. This is him setting the terms - what he will teach, what he refuses to teach, and what he is deliberately leaving out - and the "leaving out" is the design decision the entire model rests on.

## The spine

1. **The model is deliberately stripped down, and that is the whole point.** [02:38] He built it "for my daughter" - `[02:47] stripping it down to its chrome`, `[02:53] no bells and whistles no advanced theories`. [03:14] He knows some viewers will be angry it isn't `[03:19] an arsenal in your hands of high-tech weaponry`.

2. **The enemy is analysis paralysis, not ignorance.** [03:26] His highest-level concepts *do* create exceptional traders - but `[03:43] they get bogged down in all of the content and it just creates analysis paralysis`. [03:52] "Like a kid in the candy store... what do you eat first". **Every simplification later in the series traces back to this.** Read any "why didn't he include X" question through this line.

3. **Three stages: yearner -> learner -> earner.** [07:10] He is treating everyone as green horns yearning to learn, moving them to `[08:15] structured learners`, and `[08:21] at your discretion and timing you'll become an earner`. The progression is his framing of the whole series.

4. **He will not give you setups.** [07:43] `it will not be setups given to you for trades` - he will draw levels *before the fact* for study. [08:02] `do not look at that as a trade setup`. The stated reason is codependency: [10:02] depending on him `[10:09] going to stunt your growth`.

5. **Live account, on purpose, because the platform can't be faked.** [12:38] He rejects MT4 because `[12:43] frauds and fakes can use that platform to pretend that they're profitable` and `[12:56] edit their trades after the fact`. TD Ameritrade instead - [14:04] `it either is or isn't a profitable trade`. Teaching happens on TradingView. [21:53] The trade shown is real, in a live account.

6. **The target is modest and specific: 25 handles a week.** [25:15] `if you can learn how to take 25 handles out of a market like the e-mini s p`. [27:31] `at least one good setup a week`. [28:14] The framing is paying a mortgage or a car payment - not [22:24] `trying to beat the world cup`.

## Procedure taught

None. No setup is taught in this episode. The trade at [23:03] is shown as proof-of-life, not as instruction - he explicitly defers the method: [21:46] `it's just to show you that the things i'm going to be showing you are applicable`.

The only mechanical fragment is the entry philosophy: [24:28] **optimal trade entry, but forgiving** - `[24:28] you don't need to get in at this level here`, `[24:36] you don't need to be highly precise about your entries but you need to be precise about the directional bias and where it's going`.

**That is a real ranking and it matters downstream: bias precision > entry precision.**

## Reader's note

Two things here are load-bearing and easy to miss because they sound like housekeeping:

- **[24:36] bias over entry precision.** This is a ranking, and it licenses everything later about "forgiving entries". A backtest that demands precise entries is testing a different system than the one he teaches.
- **[03:43] analysis paralysis as the design constraint.** He is not simplifying because the simple version is better; he is simplifying because the complete version defeats students. That distinction matters when deciding whether a missing piece is an omission or a deliberate exclusion.
