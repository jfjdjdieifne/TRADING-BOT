# Ep07 - "Daily Bias and Consolidation Hurdles"

**In one line:** How to form a directional bias from the daily chart, what to do when there isn't one, and the intermarket "crack in correlation" that confirms a turn - **with an explicit warning that trading that divergence alone blew his accounts in the 1990s.**

## The spine

1. **Daily bias is one question: where is price drawing to?** [07:35] `you're looking at your daily chart to get a read on where it's likely to go to next ... is it going to draw higher to an old high, is it going to draw down to an old low`. [07:45] **`and until it gets to that low you stay with that bias`** - the bias persists until the draw is reached.

2. **Bias does not forbid counter-trades; it sizes them.** [10:03] `it doesn't mean that I can't take a long entry, it just means that if I do take a long entry intraday I'm going to do it with far less leverage`. With the bias: [10:15] `I'll consider using my maximum risk`.

3. **Proof of the bias, by counting candles.** [05:45] He counts the up-close days inside a large bearish leg: [05:49] `one ... two`. [06:36] `is there a lot of up closed candles during that bias being bearish? no`. The point: a bearish bias does not require every day to close down, only that most do.

4. **When price consolidates at equilibrium, bias is unreadable - and he says so.** [02:25] `it can make it very difficult to get a true reading on bias ... there's times when even me as ICT I don't have a good clear read`. [10:41] The answer is not "don't trade": [10:45] `it just means that you have to rely on these smaller time frame intraday charts and simply look for liquidity pools ... being a lot more nimble`, [11:03] `like a surgical strike - you take your handles and then run`.

5. **Intermarket relationships: the crack in correlation.** Dow, NQ and ES `[19:16] tend to move in tandem`. During a decline, [23:13] the Dow `is not willing to make that lower low where NASDAQ did, S&P did`. That crack, at the right moment, confirms accumulation.

6. **BUT: divergence alone is worthless, and he is emphatic.** [26:29] `by itself it doesn't mean anything`. [26:31] `so many students over the years have simply went in there and said oh this is magic ... and I unwillingly did the same thing when I first started observing it`. [26:38] **`I blew accounts, yes - I'm not ashamed to say it, in the '90s I blew accounts doing this very thing, just looking for these divergences`**. [26:42] `I later learned that I have to have some kind of narrative as to why this would even form`.

7. **The divergence CONFIRMS; it does not GENERATE.** [23:41] `this is something that confirms an idea that you had already established before price does what it's doing real time`. [23:49] `you're already looking for a reason to go higher`.

8. **Trading what is not in your own chart.** NQ had no fair value gap that morning. ES did. [22:00] `I don't want to trade the S&P, so I'm going to use the timing of that candle for my long entry on NASDAQ`. [22:11] `trading what's not in the chart`. [20:55] `I'm utilizing the S&P as my indicator`.

### The leader / probe order

[32:07] Learned from floor traders and George Angel: when you have no read, [32:21] `go in and buy or sell short one contract and just see what happens`.

- [33:24] `I'm trying to read what the price is doing against that order. I'm not trying to pick a spot`
- [34:02] Watch `is it easily moving away from that order, or is it lethargically moving away, or is it completely running against it`
- [30:33] The loss is `premium payments for me to get more Intel`
- [36:53] Explicitly impossible in demo: `there's no better feedback loop than to put something at risk`

## RULED by the builder: SMT is an IDEA, not a rule - not in the model

**This concept is SMT** - Smart Money Technique / Smart Money Tool. He does **not** name it here; in this episode it is only ever `cracks in correlation` [24:54] and `intermarket relationships` [42:19]. **The name arrives 22 episodes later:** ep29 [04:32] `smart money technique`, ep35 [07:14] `smt smart money technique`, ep38 [41:39] `smt smart money tool`.

**Builder's ruling, 2026-09-03: we do not need it.** It is not a rule and does not enter the model as one. Recorded here in full because it is his teaching and the corpus keeps everything (DECISIONS.md section 12) - but classified as `contextual`, not `mechanical`, per section 3.

That classification is not a demotion, it is accuracy: **his own account of it is that it is unusable as a rule.** [26:29] `by itself it doesn't mean anything`, and [26:38] trading it as one is what blew his accounts. An idea that only confirms a decision already made cannot be a trigger, and the builder's ruling and the source agree on that.

## Reader's note

**The most important thing in this episode is a warning, not a technique.**

SMT divergence is the most-copied idea in the public ICT ecosystem, and [26:38] is him saying he **blew accounts trading it in isolation**. Its role is strictly confirmatory: you must already have a narrative and a location before the divergence means anything [23:41].

For the backtester, that has a hard consequence: **SMT cannot be an entry trigger.** Coded as a signal generator it will produce exactly the account-destroying behaviour he describes. It can only ever be a filter applied to a setup that already qualified on liquidity + displacement + FVG.

Two other flags:

1. **The risk numbers are a trap for a naive reading.** He uses 3.5-4.5%; he tells students **0.25-1%** [04:52]. A backtest sized off his numbers rather than his instruction will model a far more volatile equity curve than he is recommending to anyone learning.
2. **"Trading what's not in the chart"** [22:11] means the entry signal can live in a *different instrument*. Any faithful backtest of the NQ model needs ES and YM data loaded simultaneously, or it cannot reproduce his entries.
