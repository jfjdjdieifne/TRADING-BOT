# Ep06 - "The Market Efficiency Paradigm and Institutional Order Flow"

**In one line:** The mechanical core of the entire model - **exact candle-by-candle geometry of the fair value gap, exact entry, exact stop** - plus the internal/external liquidity split that tells you where partials go and where the real target is.

**This is the most codeable episode in the corpus.** If ep02 is the map, this is the specification.

## The spine

1. **The philosophy, in two lines.** [00:33] `we look to enter longs where retail sells; we look to enter shorts where retail buys`. [00:19] `we do not trade patterns for patterns' sake, we do not trade indicator readings or momentum`.

2. **Time is the primary variable.** [03:41] `what they're specifically looking at is time and price - the most important thing is time`. [23:23] Against those who dismiss time-based charts: `algorithms, the first element they operate under is time`.

3. **THE FAIR VALUE GAP, exactly.** Three candles. Bearish version:
   - **Upper boundary = candle 1's LOW** [17:03]
   - **Lower boundary = candle 3's HIGH** [17:08]
   - The gap sits on **candle 2** [16:54]
   - Bullish is mirrored: low = candle 1's **high**, high = candle 3's **low** [22:56]

4. **Entry and stop, stated without ambiguity.** [17:21] `the easiest entry would be trading just above candle number three's high - you can put a limit order right there and be done`. [35:55] Refined live: `just go one tick above that and it removes all the doubt`. Stop: [17:34] `right above candle number one, or you can put it above candle number two`.

5. **The gap must live inside the displacement range.** [20:13] The move creates a **displacement high** and a **displacement low**; [20:29] `what you have in between that range - that's where you're going to be hunting your fair value gaps, because that's exactly where it's going to form`. [22:00] **`if there is no fair value gap in here, guess what - you don't have a trade. you wait, or go to another market.`**

6. **Displacement must CLOSE, not just wick.** [19:26] `preferably close below that`. [21:20] A `real short little drop below ... might have a fair value gap, but it also might be likely to go higher`. [21:33] `this is the secret to it here` - the difference is `a big beefy bearish candle that closes below this level`.

7. **INTERNAL vs EXTERNAL range liquidity - where partials go.** [45:33] The fair value gap inside the range is **internal range liquidity** `because it's internal relative to this low and this high`. [45:44] The stops below the old lows are **external range liquidity**. [45:52] The exit plan: `partials - internal range; external range - your trade`.

8. **The paint-roller analogy for why gaps fill.** [12:47] Rolling paint down a wall leaves `little pockets that look really porous`; you fix it by rolling back up over the same place. [13:24] `that's exactly what price does`.

### Trade management - the trailing rule

[41:26] **Do not trail the stop early.** `you use the first partial to kind of quench that desire to roll your stop - don't do that. if you do that you're probably going to get stopped out`.
[41:49] Only after an **intermediate-term low** is taken out: `then you can roll your stop to here, but not before`.

**Invalidation:** stop above candle 1 or 2. The clearest stop specification in the corpus.

## Contradicts / refines

- **Resolves ep05's displacement vagueness - partially.** Ep05 defined displacement only by analogy. Here a **testable criterion** is added: the candle must **close** beyond the level [19:26], not merely wick through [21:20]. Still no magnitude threshold, but "closes beyond" is codeable.
- **Sharpens ep02's entry from a zone to a price.** Ep02 [29:21] said sell when price goes `right up inside that area`. Here it is a **limit order one tick above candle 3's high** [35:55].
- **Answers ep02's target question properly.** Ep02 said `nearest old low or imbalance` [48:02]. Ep06 splits it: **internal** range FVG = partial; **external** range stops = the trade's target [45:52].
- **Confirms ep05's de-emphasis of order blocks.** The full specification here uses **no order block at all** - only liquidity run, displacement, FVG.

## Reader's note

**This episode is the backtest spec.** Ep02 gives the narrative, ep06 gives the numbers. Where they differ, ep06 is more precise and later.

Three things that make this codeable where earlier episodes were not:

1. **The FVG is now unambiguous geometry** - two boundaries from two named candles. No interpretation required.
2. **The entry is a limit at a computable price** (c3 high + 1 tick), and the stop is a computable price (c1 or c2 high). A backtest can be written from [35:55] and [36:07] alone.
3. **The displacement range constrains where the FVG may be found.** This is a real filter: a gap that exists *outside* the displacement high/low is not the setup [20:29]. Most naive FVG scanners ignore this and will generate trades he would never take.

The one remaining hole is unchanged from ep05: **how energetic is energetic?** "Closes beyond the level" narrows it, but a slow three-candle drift that happens to close beyond would qualify under a literal reading while clearly violating his intent. That threshold is still yours to invent, and still belongs in the ledger.
