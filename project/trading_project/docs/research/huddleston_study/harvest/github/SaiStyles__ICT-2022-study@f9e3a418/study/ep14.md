# Ep14 - A single live execution, narrated

**In one line:** Four minutes, no theory - he takes one long, narrates every decision as it happens, and shows the **scale-out and stop-to-breakeven mechanics** that no other episode states plainly.

Recorded on request [00:04] `I got requested to show how I use this model`, and addressed to a named viewer at the end [04:31] `James, there's your example`.

## The spine

1. **The setup, stated in nine seconds.** [00:08] `we had a market structure shift to the upside, I'm looking for a gap fill from Friday's close`. Entry by **limit order** into the fair value gap [00:30].

2. **Watch the candles, not the P&L - stated again.** [01:09] `I'm not watching the number underneath, that profit. that's not what I'm watching. **I'm watching the candles.**` The question he asks [01:16]: `is it constantly going towards that small little fair value gap`.

3. **The scale-out plan, decided in advance.** [02:55] `this is what I'm going to try - take six contracts off above here`. [03:02] `if it runs there I'll take six off **to fund the position**, **roll the stops to even**, and then see if I can get that limit order at 14,220`.

4. **He checks a correlated market before committing.** [03:28] `looking at e-mini S&P micro just to get a feel - looks like it wants to run as high as well ... it kind of gives me fuel or confidence`.

5. **He changes his mind and says so.** [03:49] `I'm gonna change my mind and close it here because it's getting real close to the closure of that gap. **I might be wrong**, but just for general principles`.

6. **On retail resistance thinking.** [02:33] `resistance ideas from retail are going to see that 160 and say okay I'm gonna get short ... I'm not thinking that - I'm thinking it's gonna swat, bust through the door`.

## Contradicts / refines

- **Supplies the money-management mechanics the corpus keeps gesturing at.** Ep06 [45:52] said `partials - internal range; external range - your trade` but never said *how much*. Here: **half the position**, at the first objective, then **stop to breakeven** [03:02]. First concrete scale-out ratio in the corpus.
- **Consistent with ep07's use of a correlated market**, but weaker - here ES is a confidence check [03:36], not the entry trigger it was in ep07 [22:00].
- **Shows discretion overriding the plan.** He exits early against his own stated target because of proximity to the gap [03:52]. **A backtest cannot reproduce this**, and it is worth noting that his live results include such overrides.

## Reader's note

Short and almost entirely mechanical, which makes it unusually useful.

Two things to carry into a backtest:

1. **The scale-out is 50% at the first objective, then breakeven stop** [03:02]. That single rule changes an equity curve more than most entry refinements, and it is the only place he states a ratio.
2. **He entered on a limit into the gap, not on a trigger** [00:30] - consistent with ep06 [17:21] and ep12's aggressive entry. The limit-order entry is now confirmed across three episodes as his default, not a special case.

And one honest limitation: [03:49] he closed the trade early *because he felt it was close enough*, explicitly flagging `I might be wrong`. The recorded outcome therefore reflects a discretionary exit, not the stated plan. **When his live results are used as evidence that the model works, this is the kind of gap between plan and execution that has to be acknowledged.**
