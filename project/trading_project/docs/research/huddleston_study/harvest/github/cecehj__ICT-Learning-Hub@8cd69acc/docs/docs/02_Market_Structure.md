# Market Structure

Market structure describes how price moves through highs and lows.

## Basic Terms

### Higher High (HH)

A new high above the previous swing high.

### Higher Low (HL)

A higher support level during an uptrend.

### Lower High (LH)

A lower swing high during a downtrend.

### Lower Low (LL)

A new low below the previous swing low.

## Break of Structure (BOS)

BOS occurs when price breaks a previous significant high or low, indicating continuation of the current market direction.

## Market Structure Shift (MSS)

MSS describes a potential change in market direction when price breaks an important opposing structure.
