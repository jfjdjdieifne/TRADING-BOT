#---------------------Liquidity Sweep Lab---------------------#

A Python-based quantitative research project to backtest, analyze, and statistically evaluate a liquidity sweep + retracement trading strategy.

This project is for educational and research purposes only. It does not constitute financial advice or a recommendation to trade or invest.

---

#---------------------About the Project---------------------#

Liquidity Sweep Lab is an experimental quantitative trading research project designed to investigate whether a trading setup based on:

- Liquidity sweeps
- Retracements
- Market structure
- Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
- Order Blocks
- Multiple entry confluences
- Risk/Reward management

can demonstrate a statistically significant positive expectancy over historical market data.

The project is inspired by a trading methodology described as:

> "I look for a liquidity sweep + retracement and 2 or 3 entry confluences."

Rather than assuming that the strategy is profitable, this project attempts to **test and potentially disprove the hypothesis using historical data and statistical analysis**.

---

#---------------------Project Objective---------------------#

The primary objective is to answer:

> **Does a liquidity sweep + retracement strategy provide a measurable statistical edge after accounting for risk and trading costs?**

The project will evaluate the strategy through systematic backtesting instead of relying on individual successful trades or social-media results.

---

#---------------------Strategy Concept---------------------#

The initial strategy follows this general structure:

Liquidity
    →
Liquidity Sweep
    →
Rejection / Market Reaction
    →
Retracement
    →
2–3 Entry Confluences
    →
Entry
    →
Stop Loss + Take Profit
