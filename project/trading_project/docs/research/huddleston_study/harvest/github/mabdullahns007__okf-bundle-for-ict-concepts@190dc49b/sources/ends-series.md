# Scope

Four videos released alongside the 2022 ICT Mentorship, labeled "ENDS Series" volumes/parts 1 through 4. Unlike chart-pattern lessons, this is a lecture-style series aimed at students who have already studied the free 2022 model and need guidance on actually using it.

# Contents

Part 1 sets out the purpose: ICT explains that books and teachers gave him things to look for but never a blueprint for attacking the root problems that brought him to trading. The series is framed as a transitional guide — moving the student from the way most people enter the industry toward aligning their thinking and routine with the 2022 mentorship model. Subsequent parts continue this practical-application thread, addressing how to organize one's approach, expectations, and daily practice around concepts already taught, rather than introducing new patterns.

# Key Themes

- Mindset transition from pattern-hunting to process-driven trading
- Practical application of the free 2022 mentorship model
- Root psychological problems behind trading failure
- Realistic expectations; refining craft rather than seeking exciting setups
- Building a personal roadmap around taught concepts
