# Scope

The Charter Content series is the foundational curriculum of the Inner Circle Trader channel, presenting a numbered progression of complete trading models. Each model receives an amplified lecture, a written trade plan, and an accompanying algorithmic theory session explaining the institutional logic behind price delivery.

# Contents

Thirty-four videos covering Models 1 through 13: early models on basic price action framing, Model 4 for position trading, Model 5 for intraday volatility day trading, Model 6 as the "universal" trading model with three supplementary lessons, Model 8 targeting 6% per month, Model 9's one-shot-one-kill approach, Model 10 swing trading, Model 11 day trading, Model 12 scalping, and a closing lecture applying the framework to the 2022 year.

# Key Themes

- Systematic model-building: each setup is defined by entry conditions, stop placement, and target objectives rather than discretion.
- Algorithmic delivery theory: price is treated as machine-driven, seeking liquidity pools at old highs and lows.
- Trade plans as discipline: every model pairs theory with an executable plan to remove improvisation.
- Scalability across styles, from scalping to position trading.
