# Scope

This series confronts the fantasy of instant profitability. ICT outlines what a realistic first year looks like when trading real money: small gains, inevitable losing streaks, and the emotional adjustments required when actual capital is at stake.

# Contents

Three parts covering first-year performance expectations, how losses should be budgeted for emotionally and financially, and practical guidance on position sizing and review habits that keep new traders in the game long enough to improve.

# Key Themes

- Real money changes everything: execution discipline degrades under financial pressure unless trained.
- Modest, consistent objectives (e.g., small monthly percentages) beat aggressive targets that blow accounts.
- Tracking and journaling convert first-year losses into tuition rather than waste.
