# Scope
The 2022 ICT Mentorship is a free, year-long YouTube mentorship series of roughly forty episodes. It is the primary modern-era companion to the older private mentorships, teaching the full ICT framework against live ES and NQ price action rather than static chart archives.

# Contents
- Weekly numbered episodes (001–042) following one continuous trading year.
- Tape reading practice demonstrations applied in real time.
- Counter-trend ideas — engaging moves against the prevailing dealing range.
- Recurring market reviews tying daily bias to higher-timeframe narrative.
