# Definition
The Power of Three (AMD) holds that institutional sessions, days, weeks, and even years unfold in three phases: **accumulation** (range-building near the open), **manipulation** (a false move — the *judas swing* — that runs one side's liquidity to fill large orders against retail), and **distribution** (the true directional delivery to the opposing liquidity pool). The judas swing is thus the manipulation leg: e.g., an early push above the daily/session high that reverses into a sustained decline.

# Mechanics
Applied to the daily chart, AMD frames the open: accumulation around the midnight or 8:30/9:30 ET opens, a manipulation often during premarket or the London/NY killzones ([/concepts/killzones-and-time.md]), then distribution into the afternoon toward the draw on liquidity ([/concepts/liquidity.md]). Opening range gaps anchor the day's architecture ([/concepts/opening-range-gaps.md]); the manipulation typically purges a pool and leaves displacement — an FVG or MSS — marking the transition ([/concepts/fair-value-gap.md], [/concepts/market-structure.md]). Dealing-range context tells you which direction the distribution should run ([/concepts/premium-discount.md]).

# Application
Traders use AMD for session profiling: expect the first move from a key open to fail, wait for the sweep plus displacement, then trade the distribution leg with targets at the opposing session/daily extreme. It also defines risk rules — entries taken *after* manipulation, never during ([/concepts/risk-management.md]). The 2022 mentorship demonstrates the daily-bias version of this workflow repeatedly ([/sources/mentorship-2022.md]).

# Evolution
Rooted in ICT's earliest market-maker narratives (the forex market maker series and 2016 mentorship teach open-driven profiles); "judas swing" is older vocabulary that persists unchanged ([/sources/2016-mentorship-month-04.md]). Modern eras attach AMD to specific opens — midnight opening range, 9:30 RTH open — with 2025–2026 lectures timing phases via macros and opening-range probing rather than vague "session" language ([/sources/lecture-series-2025.md], [/sources/smart-money-lectures-2026.md]). Not every day follows AMD cleanly; ICT himself flags flat, holiday, and news-dominated days as exceptions.
