# Scope

One of ICT's most referenced early series. It teaches the Market Maker Buy and Sell Models: how smart money accumulates positions at lows, manipulates price to run stops and pair orders, then distributes into highs. Every later ICT concept (killzones, OTE, order blocks) builds on this framework.

# Contents

Five volumes walking through the full buy-side and sell-side profiles, stop hunts and liquidity runs, the relationship between retail order flow and institutional fills, and chart applications of the model on major pairs.

# Key Themes

- Price is engineered: markets move to where orders rest, not where indicators suggest.
- AMD cycle — accumulation, manipulation, distribution — as the repeating intraday and weekly structure.
- Thinking institutionally: stops below lows are buy-limit liquidity for smart money.
