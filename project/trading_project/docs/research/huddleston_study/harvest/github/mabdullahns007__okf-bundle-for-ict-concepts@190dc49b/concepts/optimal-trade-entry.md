# Definition

Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) is ICT's method of entering a trending move after price retraces into the 62%–79% band (with 70.5% as the midpoint emphasis) of the most recent dealing range — the impulse leg established by a break in market structure.

# Mechanics

1. Identify a market structure shift (see [/concepts/market-structure.md]) that creates a new impulse leg.
2. Measure the fib retracement of that leg; the OTE zone is 62%–79%, with 70.5% the "sweet spot."
3. Look for a lower-timeframe PD array inside the zone — FVG, order block, or breaker (see [/concepts/fair-value-gap.md], [/concepts/order-blocks.md]) — to refine the exact entry.
4. Stop goes beyond the extreme of the impulse (or the liquidity sweep); target is external liquidity / opposing range extreme.
5. Confluence raises quality: killzone timing ([/concepts/killzones-and-time.md]), premium/discount alignment ([/concepts/premium-discount.md]), and a liquidity raid preceding the leg ([/concepts/liquidity.md]).

# Application

ICT teaches OTE as a continuation model, not a reversal tool — you buy discounts in a bullish delivery and sell premiums in bearish. The [OTE Pattern Recognition Series](/sources/ote-pattern-recognition-series.md) drills the pattern through twenty volumes of chart repetition, stressing that the fib is a framing tool rather than magic lines. The [Forex Market Maker Primer](/sources/forex-market-maker-primer.md) and [2022 Mentorship](/sources/mentorship-2022.md) apply it on live pairs.

# Evolution

Later material (2024–2025 mentorships, [2025 Lecture Series](/sources/lecture-series-2025.md)) increasingly subordinates OTE to time-based models like Silver Bullet, where the fib refines entries within a macro window rather than driving setup selection.
