# Concepts

* [Overview](/overview.md) - synthesis of the ICT framework and its evolution

* [Fair Value Gap (FVG)](/concepts/fair-value-gap.md) - A three-candle inefficiency in price delivery where a gap between the first candle's wick and the third candle's wick marks an imbalance the algorithm is said to rebalance, serving as ICT's primary entry array.
* [](/concepts/index.md) - 
* [Killzones and Time](/concepts/killzones-and-time.md) - ICT's thesis that specific time windows (London/NY killzones, macros, midnight open) drive algorithmic price delivery more than indicators or patterns.
* [Liquidity](/concepts/liquidity.md) - The pools of resting stop orders (buyside and sellside) that ICT claims smart money engineers price toward, forming the basis of runs, purges, and draw-on-liquidity bias.
* [Market Structure](/concepts/market-structure.md) - ICT's fractal framework of swing highs and lows, breaks of structure, and changes in the state of price delivery used to define bias across time frames.
* [Opening Range Gaps](/concepts/opening-range-gaps.md) - ICT's framework of opening prices and gaps — midnight opening range, RTH opening range, NWOG/NYOG/NDOG — used as daily reference architecture and rebalance magnets.
* [Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)](/concepts/optimal-trade-entry.md) - ICT's fibonacci-based entry framework that targets the 62-79% retracement of an impulse leg as the highest-probability zone to rejoin a trend.
* [Order Blocks](/concepts/order-blocks.md) - ICT's family of institutional footprints — the last opposing candle before displacement — extended across breaker, mitigation, rejection, propulsion, and suspension block variants.
* [Power of Three (AMD)](/concepts/power-of-three.md) - ICT's accumulation–manipulation–distribution template describing how smart money builds positions, runs liquidity via the judas swing, then delivers price to its true objective.
* [Premium, Discount & the PD Array Matrix](/concepts/premium-discount.md) - ICT's dealing-range framework that grades every price level as premium or discount around equilibrium, organized in later years into a ranked matrix of algorithmic price-delivery arrays.
* [Risk Management](/concepts/risk-management.md) - ICT's risk doctrine — stops beyond liquidity pools, partials at short-term targets, strict position sizing, and one-shot-one-kill selectivity.
* [Trading Psychology](/concepts/trading-psychology.md) - ICT's recurring mindset teachings — routine, time management, fear of being wrong, demo-to-live progression, and realistic first-year expectations.
* [Turtle Soup](/concepts/turtle-soup.md) - ICT's false-breakout fade setup — entering against a raid of a prior session high or low once liquidity is swept and price rejects back into range.
