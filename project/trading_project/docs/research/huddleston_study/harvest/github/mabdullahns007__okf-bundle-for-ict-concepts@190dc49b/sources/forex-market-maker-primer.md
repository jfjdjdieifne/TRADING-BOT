# Scope

This primer course is the on-ramp to the ICT forex curriculum. It assumes no prior knowledge of the methodology and builds a complete working framework: what to focus on as a new trader, how risk is framed, and how the market maker's model explains price delivery across sessions.

# Contents

Twenty-four lessons including what new traders should focus on, risk management considerations, essentials of ICT market structure, the four forex killzones (Asian, London, New York, London Close), weekly bias, time and price theory, accumulation-manipulation-distribution, the Asian range, the Judas swing, daily bias, key swing points, money management, the Smart Money Technique (SMT), the ATM method, an Optimal Trade Entry primer, target selection, journaling and trade psychology, COT insights, anticipating explosive moves, higher time frame concepts, and swing trading secrets.

# Key Themes

- Time and price together: killzones define when setups are valid; structure defines where.
- The AMD cycle and Judas Swing as the engine of intraday manipulation.
- Position sizing and journaling treated as core skills, not afterthoughts.
