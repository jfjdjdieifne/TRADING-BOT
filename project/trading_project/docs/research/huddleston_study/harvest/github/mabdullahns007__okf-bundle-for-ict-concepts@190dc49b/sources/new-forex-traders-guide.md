# Scope

A short but essential orientation for beginners. Rather than teaching setups, ICT addresses the mindset, expectations, and study habits that determine whether a new trader survives long enough to become profitable. It is the recommended starting point of the channel's forex content.

# Contents

Five parts (W.E.N.T. series) covering why most new traders fail, what to focus on learning first, how to approach risk from day one, the role of demo versus live trading, and how to build a sustainable learning path through the channel's material.

# Key Themes

- Realistic expectations: no strategy guarantees profits; process matters more than outcomes.
- Simplification: focus on a small number of concepts applied repeatedly.
- Survival first: capital preservation and small risk precede aggressive growth.
