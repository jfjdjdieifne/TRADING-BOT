# Scope

A retrospective, mentorship-style series rather than a technical course. ICT speaks directly about the trading journey: the errors that cost him years, the principles that finally produced consistency, and how a new trader can shortcut that path.

# Contents

Four parts covering early-career mistakes (overtrading, oversized risk), the importance of process and patience over prediction, how to study price action efficiently, and the personal discipline habits that support long-term survival in the markets.

# Key Themes

- Experience is compressible: learning from another trader's documented failures accelerates growth.
- Psychology and risk discipline outweigh setup knowledge.
- Longevity is the real edge; the market rewards those still standing.
