# Scope
The ICT 2024 Mentorship is a concentrated lecture series (~50 episodes) running from early August through December 2024. It is heavily NQ-futures oriented and leans toward trade management psychology, tape reading, and condition-filtering rather than re-teaching basics.

# Contents
- Numbered daily lectures (Lectures 1–21) across August, then dated sessions.
- Live NQ execution including a "100k OSOK" (one shot one kill) challenge trade.
- How to trade ICT FVGs correctly; how ICT picks winning FVGs and order blocks.
- High-resistance vs low-resistance liquidity run conditions — identification and navigation.
- Seek & destroy tape-reading traps and how to avoid them; news-release tape reading.
- Premarket concepts, missed-entry management, limit-order "volatility pinball" drills.
- Micros vs minis, CFDs vs futures, decoupled markets, NFP sessions.
