# Scope
First installment of the 2016 ICT Private Mentorship core content (December teaching cycle). Eight lessons establishing the mental model of how markets are engineered and how institutional order flow conditions retail traders before and during price delivery.

# Contents
- Elements of a trade setup — the components every high-probability idea must contain.
- How market makers condition traders — engineering losses to fuel moves.
- What to focus on right now — prioritizing study over indicators.
- Equilibrium vs discount / equilibrium vs premium — framing price within a dealing range.
- Fair valuation — where price is objectively cheap or expensive relative to the range.
- Liquidity runs — why stops above highs and below lows are sought out.
- Impulse price swings in market structure — how smart money leaves footprints.

# Key Themes
- Premium vs discount arrays: buy below equilibrium, sell above it — the seed of [/concepts/premium-discount.md].
- Market maker buy/sell model logic: accumulation, manipulation (judas swing), distribution.
- Liquidity runs as engineered stop hunts rather than random noise.
- Reading impulse swings as evidence of institutional sponsorship.
