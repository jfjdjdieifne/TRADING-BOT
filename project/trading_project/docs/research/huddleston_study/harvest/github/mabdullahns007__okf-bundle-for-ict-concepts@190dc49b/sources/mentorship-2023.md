# Scope
The 2023 ICT Mentorship is the largest of the free YouTube mentorships (~100 episodes). It pairs near-daily ES and NQ market reviews with dedicated lectures introducing several models that became canonical in the ICT canon.

# Contents
- Live tape reading sessions (AM/PM) around CPI, FOMC, NFP, and Fed testimony.
- Recurring ES/NQ market reviews building a daily-bias narrative.
- NWOG — New Week Opening Gap — two-part lecture; NDOG — New Day Opening Gap.
- ICT Silver Bullet time-based trading model introduction and evolving examples.
- The "sick sister consolidation" model, TGIF setup, and "one trading setup for life."
- Advanced gap theory, breaker theory, algorithmic price delivery time macros.
- ICT Reaper PD array introduction and Ma Deuce model review.
- Drawdown mitigation homework and journaling/proper-learning lectures.
