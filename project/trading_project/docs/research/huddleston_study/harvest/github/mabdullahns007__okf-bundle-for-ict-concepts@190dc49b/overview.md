# ICT Channel Knowledge Bundle — Overview

This bundle distills ~954 video transcripts from the [raw corpus](../../raw/ict-channel/) of the **ICT channel** (The Inner Circle Trader, Michael J. Huddleston) into an OKF v0.2 knowledge base: 37 source series summaries, core concept pages, and step-based playbooks.

## The core thesis

ICT teaches that markets are driven by an "algorithmic" delivery mechanism engineered around **liquidity**: price is drawn toward pools of resting stops (buyside/sellside) rather than reacting to indicators. The trader's job is to read where price is being drawn ([draw on liquidity](/concepts/liquidity.md)), wait for it to reach a premium or discount array ([premium/discount](/concepts/premium-discount.md)), and enter precisely during time windows when the algorithm is most active ([killzones & time](/concepts/killzones-and-time.md)).

## The framework in one chain

1. **Time first** — killzones, macro windows, opens. See [killzones-and-time](/concepts/killzones-and-time.md), [opening-range-gaps](/concepts/opening-range-gaps.md).
2. **Bias second** — daily direction from HTF draw on liquidity. See [daily-bias-workflow](/playbooks/daily-bias-workflow.md).
3. **Location third** — premium/discount within dealing ranges; PD arrays (FVGs, order blocks). See [premium-discount](/concepts/premium-discount.md), [fair-value-gap](/concepts/fair-value-gap.md), [order-blocks](/concepts/order-blocks.md).
4. **Trigger last** — OTE retracement, turtle soup raid-fade, silver bullet FVG. See the [playbooks](playbooks/index.md).
5. **Risk throughout** — stops beyond liquidity, one-shot-one-kill selectivity. See [risk-management](/concepts/risk-management.md).

## Evolution across eras

- **2016–2017 mentorships** laid the foundation: market maker models, block taxonomy, IPDA data ranges, seasonality, swing and short-term modules.
- **Forex-era series** (market maker primer, scout-sniper, trading plan development) formalized AMD/judas swing and precision entries.
- **2022–2024 mentorships** pivoted hard to index futures (NQ/ES): tape reading, live executions, high-resistance liquidity runs, trade management psychology.
- **2025–2026 lectures** introduced refinements and new vocabulary: *first-presented FVG*, *PD array matrix*, *midnight opening range / NWOG / NDOG*, *Venom model*, *chain of custody of price*, *RTH ORG* probing.

## Notable tensions in the material

- Terminology drift: "order block" definitions broaden over the years; blocks multiply into breaker/mitigation/rejection/propulsion/suspension variants without always consistent criteria. See [order-blocks](/concepts/order-blocks.md).
- The 2016 content emphasizes patience and small daily goals; later execution videos show aggressive intraday scalping — a style shift worth noting, not necessarily a contradiction.
- ICT repeatedly dismisses retail tools ("market structure is not it") while his own structure concepts remain central; the framing differs more than the mechanics.
