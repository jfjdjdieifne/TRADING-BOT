# Scope

Six videos from the 2026 "Price Action Chronicles" series, focused on Nasdaq (NQ) futures with one Non-Farm Payroll case study (August 7, 2026). The material is current-era ICT: short, focused lessons built around recent live conditions rather than a structured curriculum.

# Contents

The series opens with how to frame trading sessions when the market is in high-resistance liquidity-run conditions, then continues with navigation tactics for those environments. A Non-Farm Payroll case study applies the framework to a news day. Later episodes cover "the science of anticipation in price" — projecting where price is likely to be drawn before it arrives — and two episodes on the Market-on-Close (MOC) macro: how the buying/selling imbalance at MOC creates predictable runs, and how to trade the 10-30 minute PM session window as a standalone model.

# Key Themes

- High-resistance liquidity runs and session framing under them
- Anticipation of draw on liquidity rather than reaction
- Market-on-close macro windows as a complete intraday model
