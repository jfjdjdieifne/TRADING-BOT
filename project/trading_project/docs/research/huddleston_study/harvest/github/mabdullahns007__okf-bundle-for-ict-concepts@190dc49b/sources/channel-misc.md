# Scope

A mixed archive of roughly 274 videos from the ICT (Inner Circle Trader / Michael J. Huddleston) YouTube channel that do not belong to a named series. Coverage stretches from October 2017 live New York sessions through the July-August 2022 index futures reviews, the 2023 tape-reading livestreams, 2024 NQ/ES executions, and into 2025-2026 uploads. Formats include market reviews, live session commentary, pattern-recognition drills, "silent" no-audio execution examples, and short personal or community posts.

# Contents

- **Market reviews and commentary** — recurring daily/weekly reviews of ES, NQ, DXY, EURUSD, GBPUSD, and Bitcoin, often framing dealing ranges, draw on liquidity, and higher-timeframe order flow.
- **Live sessions and tape readings** — real-time session streams (e.g., March 2023 tape readings, January 2024 price-action livestreams) narrating algorithmic price delivery around news events.
- **Live executions** — full trade walkthroughs: turtle soup shorts at lunch macros, judas swing entries on ES, premium wick entries on NQ, silver bullet and FVG-based fills.
- **Pattern recognition drills** — OTE drills across EURUSD, USDCAD, gold, USDJPY in New York session.
- **Teaching supplements** — lectures on order blocks, IPDA vs Wyckoff, power of 3, Friday Asian range, and psychology pieces ("if I had to restart at 20", breaking bad habits).

# Key Themes

- Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) anticipation before patterns complete
- Killzone-timed scalps; lunch and final-hour macro windows
- Turtle soup, judas swing, silver bullet, breaker and FVG entries
- Buyside/sellside liquidity purges and reversion
- SMT divergence between correlated markets for accumulation/distribution reads
- Market maker buy/sell models; PD arrays and consequent encroachment
- Avoiding NFP weeks; trading without bias; time-of-day theory
