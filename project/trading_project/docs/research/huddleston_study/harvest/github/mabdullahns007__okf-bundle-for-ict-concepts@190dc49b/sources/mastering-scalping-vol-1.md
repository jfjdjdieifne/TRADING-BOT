# Scope

A compact three-volume series on scalping the forex market with ICT concepts. It focuses on extracting small, consistent moves during high-liquidity windows while keeping risk tightly defined — the intraday complement to swing-oriented models.

# Contents

Three volumes covering the scalping framework: session selection and timing, identifying short-term structure shifts for entries, stop placement relative to manipulation lows/highs, and scaling out of quick targets.

# Key Themes

- Timing dominates in scalping: only the killzones offer the volatility worth risking.
- Lower time frame noise is filtered through higher time frame bias.
- Small risk, defined targets: scalps succeed through consistency of execution, not home runs.
