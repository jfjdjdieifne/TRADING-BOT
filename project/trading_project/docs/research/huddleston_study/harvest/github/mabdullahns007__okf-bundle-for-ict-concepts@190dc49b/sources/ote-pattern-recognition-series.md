# Scope

A pure repetition series: nineteen volumes of guided chart reading designed to train the eye to spot Optimal Trade Entry setups quickly and consistently. Unlike lecture-based courses, this series emphasizes screen time and pattern familiarity over theory.

# Contents

Volumes 1 through 20 (vol. 11 absent from the channel), each walking through live or historical charts to identify impulse legs, market structure breaks, retracements into the 62–79% Fibonacci zone, and subsequent expansion legs.

# Key Themes

- Deliberate practice: OTE mastery comes from seeing hundreds of examples, not memorizing rules.
- Structure confirmation: entries are only valid after a break in short-term market structure.
- Fib as a framing tool: the 62%, 70.5%, and 79% levels define the entry band, not magic lines.
