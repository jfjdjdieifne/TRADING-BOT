# Scope

Twelve videos numbered from the 2023-2024 mentorship uploads, all recorded live during December 2023 - August 2024. Markets are almost exclusively Nasdaq (NQ) with some ES coverage. Formats include full narrated executions, macro-window scalps, and commentary on algorithmic/high-frequency tape behavior.

# Contents

Notable entries include live executions on the December 2023 Non-Farm Payroll release, a final trading day of 2023 commentary, final-hour MOC macro shorts (January 17, 2024), sessions discussing algorithmic high-frequency trading and its opposite-direction effects, bias-free micro scalping (May 6, 2024), a premarket short (May 13, 2024), a lunch-macro trade (June 10, 2024), a June 24, 2024 turtle soup short, and an order-block-to-discount NDOG trade (August 27, 2024). One video examines what the "1 day trader on YouTube" could do with a funded account.

# Key Themes

- Turtle soup entries at lunch and session extremes
- Macro windows (final hour, MOC, lunch) as execution triggers
- Trading around scheduled news (NFP) and why ICT often avoids it
- Premium wick and inversion FVG entry tactics
- Stop placement at consequent encroachment; partial scaling
- Reading algorithmic/high-frequency order flow direction
