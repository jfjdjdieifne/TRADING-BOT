# Scope

This series addresses the gap between knowing ICT concepts and trading them consistently. It walks step-by-step through assembling a written trading plan: defining your market and sessions, establishing daily and weekly bias, specifying exact entry triggers, sizing positions, and managing trades to targets.

# Contents

Seven parts that progressively construct a complete plan — from foundational decision-making and market selection through bias formulation, entry rules, risk parameters, trade management, and review/refinement of the finished plan.

# Key Themes

- A trading plan must be specific enough to remove in-the-moment decisions.
- Risk definition precedes entry definition; position size derives from stop placement.
- Journaling and periodic review turn a static plan into an evolving process.
