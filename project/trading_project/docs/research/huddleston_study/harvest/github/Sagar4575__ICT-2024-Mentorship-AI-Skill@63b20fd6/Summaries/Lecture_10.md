# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 10 (August 16, 2024)

## Core Technical Concepts & Topics

### 1. Weekly Range Profiling & TGIF
- To profile the weekly range, measure from the lowest low to the highest high (on an up week) using a Fibonacci tool to find the 20% and 30% retracement levels.
- **The TGIF setup:** If the weekly range has had an elongated, significant directional run and then achieves a 20-30% retracement of that total weekly range by Thursday afternoon or Friday morning, the weekly "Power 3" objective is complete.
- **Trading Environment Post-TGIF:** After the weekly objective is met, the market tends to become "fickle," choppy, and range-bound, settling into the upper or lower third of the weekly candle. ICT avoids trading in this environment (often entirely skipping Fridays when this happens) because clean, fast setups are rare.

### 2. Opening Range vs. Opening Range Gap
- **Opening Range:** The range established between the highest high and lowest low within the first 30 minutes of regular trading hours (9:30 AM to 10:00 AM EST).
- **Opening Range Gap:** The difference/void between the *previous day's 5:00 PM settlement price* and the *current day's 9:30 AM opening price*.
- *Rule:* Carry forward any unfilled opening range gaps for three days. They act as highly influential key levels. Keep charts "naked" and manage these levels on a physical notepad to reduce visual clutter and emotional bias.

### 3. Tape Reading & Data Collection
- The goal of tape reading is to study price delivery without the pressure of live money or demo buttons. 
- *Methodology:* Identify a setup, record the exact time, the target, and how long it takes to reach it. Do not ignore failed setups; they provide the most valuable learning data.
- It is unrealistic to expect real profitability within the first six months. Traders must build a data set of real-time observations to determine their personal strike rate and build a "model" that fits their personality.

### 4. Minimum Range Threshold & Running Down Equity
- When hunting for setups, do not demand perfection, but demand a minimum range. For NQ, a valid setup should afford a 20-handle projection between the entry trigger and the obvious draw on liquidity. This makes capturing 10-15 handles mathematically reasonable.
- **Running Down Equity (Partialing):** If you are in a profitable trade and price passes a short-term extreme (like an old high or low) that is at least 50% of the way to the final Terminus target, you *must* take a partial profit. Move the stop-loss to protect the remaining balance.

### 5. High-Resistance Liquidity Runs vs. Clean Price Action
- **Clean Price Action:** Characterized by obvious displacement, one clear draw on liquidity, and speed (like grabbing a green apple out of a bucket of red ones). 
- **High-Resistance Liquidity Run:** Occurs on choppy, indecisive days where price moves back and forth with overlapping candle bodies. When price has chopped down into a low multiple times, returning to take that low is difficult because the market lacks clear momentum.
- *Manual Intervention:* If the market completes a standard retracement and then slowly drifts higher to break new highs without news drivers, this is often manual market-maker intervention on low volume.

### 6. The Base Entry & Stop Protocol (For Practice)
- To build a baseline for tape reading, use the **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** as the entry mechanism.
- *Entry Rules:* Buy one tick below the high of a discount bullish FVG. Sell short one tick above the low of a premium bearish FVG.
- *Stop Loss Placement:* A stop loss must be finite. Place the stop loss beyond the swing low or swing high that initiated the displacement creating the FVG.

## Important Definitions
- **TGIF Retracement:** The 20% to 30% retracement of the total weekly range that tends to happen late Thursday or on Friday, completing the weekly candle profile.
- **Opening Range Gap:** The void between the previous session's 5:00 PM close and today's 9:30 AM open.
- **Running Down Equity:** The protocol of securing partial profits once the trade travels halfway to the target and overtakes an internal liquidity pool.
- **Terminus:** The final, intended destination of a trade idea.
