# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 20: Psychology, Discipline, & High-Resistance Frameworks

## 1. Trade Psychology & Eliminating Angst

### The Half-Size De-stressor
*   **The Dilemma:** Developing traders often enter a position, see the market stall or start to look "heavy," and immediately panic. They debate whether to close the full position, risking missing a move if they are right.
*   **The Solution:** The absolute instant you feel that anxiety rising and start questioning the technical validity of the setup, **cut the trade size in half**.
*   **The Logic:** You reduce the emotional and financial pressure. If it was a 1-contract trade, close it entirely and "tape read" the rest. This preserves capital and trains you to monitor price objectively without the distortion of realized drawdowns.

### Sitting Still (The Win-Win)
*   You never lose money sitting on the sidelines. If the framework requires forced 50/50 guesses (e.g., conflicting PD Arrays, tightly clustered old gaps), it is considered **High Resistance**. 
*   **The Spider Web Analogy:** When New Day Opening Gaps, New Week Opening Gaps, and old highs/lows are heavily layered over a small price range, they act like a spider web. Price (the fly) gets tangled and chops back and forth. You want to trade when the fly escapes the web and heads straight for the sugar (open liquidity).

## 2. Risk/Reward and Stop-Loss Management

### The 5-to-1 Gearing Model
*   The goal is to structure trades where the target yields five times the initial risk (e.g., risk 18 handles to make 90 handles).
*   With a 5:1 reward-to-risk ratio (gearing), you can afford to lose over 70% of your trades and still be net profitable. It removes the pressure of needing a high win rate.

### Stop Management & Commission Costs
*   When a trade begins moving in your favor, identify the distance to your **First Partial** (usually a logical short-term high/low or the midpoint of an inefficiency).
*   When price moves **halfway** to your first partial target, move your stop loss to cover entry **plus commission costs** (e.g., +0.5 to +1 handle). Do not just move it to "break even," as closing at break even still results in a net loss due to fees.

## 3. Advanced Concepts

### SMT Divergence (Smart Money Tool)
*   Compare correlated assets like NQ (NASDAQ) and ES (S&P 500).
*   If NQ makes a higher high, but ES fails to make a higher high at the exact same time, that divergence is an SMT. 
*   It does *not* necessarily mean an instant trend reversal, but it strongly indicates that bullish momentum is waning and the probability of a pullback or sideways chop is elevated.

### The Institutional Order Entry Drill (Revisited)
*   When a Breakaway Gap or an FVG is tapped by only a single tick (or minimal ticks) and immediately aggressively rejects, leaving almost the entire inefficiency open.
*   The stop loss goes one tick below the low of the *imbalance candle* (the middle candle of the 3-bar FVG sequence).
