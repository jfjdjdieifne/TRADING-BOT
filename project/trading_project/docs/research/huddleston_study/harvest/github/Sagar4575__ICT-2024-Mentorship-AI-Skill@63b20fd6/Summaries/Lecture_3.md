# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 3 Summary (August 7, 2024)

## The Afternoon Session Context
- While beginners should focus primarily on the AM session (7:00 AM - 11:00 AM EST), the PM (Afternoon) session has distinct characteristics and specific trade setups.
- **When to trade the PM Session:** If the economic calendar is devoid of high-impact or medium-impact news drivers during the morning (AM) session, expect lackluster, choppy, or "Judas Swing" price action in the morning. In such scenarios, the Afternoon session (often triggered around 1:00 PM or 1:30 PM EST) usually provides cleaner, highly probable setups.

## Gap Theory: NDOG and NWOG
The most magnetic draws on liquidity are time-based gaps that represent true imbalances (areas where no trading occurred). 

### New Day Opening Gap (NDOG)
- **Formation:** Formed by the difference between the 5:00 PM EST market close and the 6:00 PM EST market reopen (applies to Index markets like Nasdaq/S&P).
- **Life Cycle:** 5 trading days.
- **Application:** Keep a running log of the NDOGs from the past 5 trading days. They act as "black holes" or magnets pulling price toward them, especially if they remain untested.

### New Week Opening Gap (NWOG)
- **Formation:** Formed by the difference between Friday's 5:00 PM EST close and Sunday's 6:00 PM EST reopen.
- **Life Cycle:** 5 trading weeks.
- **Application:** Because it spans a larger timeframe, it exerts a stronger gravitational pull on price than an NDOG.

### Gap Quadrants and Consequent Encroachment (CE)
- **Consequent Encroachment:** The exact midpoint (50%) of a gap.
- Divide any gap into quadrants (25%, 50%, 75%).
- A highly sensitive FVG or Gap will hold price exactly at its Consequent Encroachment or one of its quadrants. 
- *Strength Signature:* If a candle approaches a gap but fails to even touch its Consequent Encroachment before reversing, it signals extreme underlying strength/heaviness in the opposing direction.

## Engineering Liquidity & The Judas Swing
- The algorithm creates relative equal highs and lows specifically to trick "street money" (retail traders).
- **Example Scenario:** If price has clustered NDOGs *below* it, the true algorithmic draw is downward. However, at the start of an hour (7:00, 8:00, 9:00), the market may initially spike upward to take out short-term buy-side liquidity (relative equal highs). 
- This upward spike is the **Judas Swing**. It induces retail traders to go long on a breakout, right before the market aggressively reverses to seek the true draw (the NDOG below).

## Holding Trades Longer
To catch 50 to 100+ handle runs, you must marry a short-term entry with a long-term narrative:
1. **Identify the Draw:** You must have an overarching reason for price to expand significantly, such as an old NWOG or clustered NDOGs deep below/above current price.
2. **Accept Drawdown:** Holding a trade requires stomaching intraday retracements. You cannot panic-close your trade every time a 1-minute candle moves against you.
3. **Pyramid Entries:** Use a scaled approach (e.g., 6 contracts at the first entry, 4 at a retracement). This blends your average price and gives you psychological leeway to hold through "heat."

## Psychology & "Dough Repair"
- **The "Dough Repair Kit":** Treat your losses like making a hole in pizza dough. You don't throw it away; you just fold it, roll it out, and start again. A stop-out is a normal business expense.
- **Avoid Street Money Noise:** Ignore social media, signal services, and the need to show off profits. Trading is a solitary endeavor aimed at financial independence, not social validation. 
- **Demo / Paper Trade:** Do not gamble live capital until you have built a baseline of trust by tape-reading and demo trading for months.
