# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 7 Summary (August 13, 2024)

## Trading High-Impact News Days
- **CPI, PPI, and NFP:** On days when high-impact economic data drops (typically at 8:30 AM EST), ICT strongly advises against trying to predict or trade the initial volatility. 
- **The "Hurricane" Analogy:** The initial news release behaves like a hurricane. You cannot stand in front of it. Let the volatility hit the market, sweep liquidity on both sides, and wait for the dust to settle before looking for technical setups.
- **When to Execute:** On these days, it is best to avoid the 9:30 AM equities open entirely and instead look for setups specifically around the **10:00 AM Silver Bullet** hour or the subsequent **Macros**.

## Top-Down Analysis & Defining the Draw on Liquidity
- **Start at the Top:** Before looking at the 1-minute chart, you must look at the Weekly and Daily charts.
- **Identify Daily/Weekly Targets:** Look for obvious levels of inefficiency on the higher timeframes, such as a Daily Volume Imbalance, a Weekly Fair Value Gap, or prominent old highs/lows.
- **The Intraday Gravitation:** The intraday price action (e.g., the run after the 9:30 AM open) will often act as a direct magnet moving toward those Daily or Weekly objectives. If price has already moved 50-100 handles to reach that daily objective right at the open, **do not chase the price**. Wait for the algorithm to set up a new framework.

## The Macro Times (The Algorithmic Spooling)
- **What is a Macro?** A macro is a specific 20-minute time window where the algorithmic delivery of price becomes highly efficient and accelerated ("spooling").
- **Key Morning Macros:** 
  - 10:50 AM to 11:10 AM EST
  - 11:50 AM to 12:10 PM EST
- **What Happens in a Macro?** During these 20-minute windows, the algorithm will consistently seek one of two things:
  1. **Inefficiency:** It will reprice into a Fair Value Gap or Volume Imbalance.
  2. **Liquidity:** It will run above short-term highs (Buy Side) or below short-term lows (Sell Side).
- **Application:** You do not need to trade all day. You can set an alarm for 10:45 AM, observe the setup, and execute during the 10:50 AM macro, knowing the algorithm is programmed to quickly reach for a nearby liquidity pool or inefficiency.

## Entry Techniques & PD Arrays
- **The Institutional Order Flow Entry Drill:** 
  - This is an entry technique utilized when you anticipate a Fair Value Gap will remain *open* (acting as a Breakaway Gap).
  - Instead of waiting for price to retrace deep into the gap, you place your limit order at the very edge of the gap (e.g., the low of the candle creating the bearish gap, or the high of the candle creating the bullish gap).
  - A more conservative, guaranteed fill is placing the limit order exactly at **Consequent Encroachment** (the midpoint) between the high and low of the gap.
- **Refining the Order Block:**
  - An Order Block is not always just the single last down-close candle before an up-move.
  - It can be a series of **consecutive** down-close candles.
  - In a series of consecutive down-close candles, the **Opening Price** of the highest candle in that sequence is the true "change in state of delivery." Once price trades above that opening price, the entire sequence is validated as a Bullish Order Block. Any return to that opening price or the upper half of those candle bodies can be used for a long entry.

## Trading Psychology: Knowing Your "Kryptonite"
- **The "Traffic Jam" Market:** ICT detests days where the market slowly grinds in one direction without meaningful retracements, or chops sideways aimlessly like a traffic jam. He considers this his "kryptonite."
- **The Napkin Test:** If you cannot draw your trading setup (your entry, your stop loss, your target, and your draw on liquidity) cleanly on a napkin using simple lines, you do not have a defined model. You cannot trade what you cannot clearly articulate.
- **Permission to Sit Out:** If the day's price action does not cleanly fit the setup you drew on your napkin, you have won by choosing *not* to trade. Do not force a trade out of boredom, as the resulting loss will cause emotional tilt that bleeds into the next trading day.
