# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 5 Summary (August 9, 2024)

## The Asian Session (6:00 PM to 9:00 PM EST)
- **The Restart Gap:** Indices pause trading from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM EST. When the market reopens at 6:00 PM, the difference between the 5:00 PM close and the 6:00 PM open creates the New Day Opening Gap (NDOG).
- **Initial Liquidity Engineering:** Between 6:00 PM and 7:00 PM, do not trade. Watch the market gyrate away from the NDOG to take out a minor short-term high (establishing Initial Buy-Side Liquidity) and a minor short-term low (establishing Initial Sell-Side Liquidity).
- **The 7:00 PM Trigger:** At 7:00 PM EST, the Asian session algorithm officially begins. A high-probability signature is price retracing *back* to the 6:00 PM NDOG exactly at or shortly after 7:00 PM, finding support/resistance there, and then aggressively displacing toward the previously established initial liquidity pools.

## The London Session & Regular Trading Hours
- **London Opening Range:** The opening range for the London session is established between 12:00 AM (Midnight) and 12:30 AM EST. 
- **New York Opening Range:** The opening range for the New York regular session is the first 30 minutes of the equities open, from 9:30 AM to 10:00 AM EST.
- **RTH Gaps:** If you switch your chart from Electronic Trading Hours (ETH) to Regular Trading Hours (RTH), you will see massive gaps at the 9:30 AM open because overnight data is hidden.
- **The 70% Rule:** If the market has a massive gap lower at the 9:30 AM RTH open, there is a 70% probability that price will retrace upward to the *midpoint* of that gap before continuing its downward expansion. Trading back to the mid-gap is a highly reliable setup.

## Targeting with Measured Moves
- **Fibonacci Extensions for Exits:** When the market breaks out, use the preceding price leg (the swing high to swing low just before the run) to project targets.
- **-1 and -1.5 Standard Deviations:** A -1 extension is a perfect 100% measured move. A -1.5 extension indicates extreme algorithmic momentum. 
- **The Sweet Spot:** Do not be greedy and aim for the absolute top. ICT recommends placing limit orders between the -1 and -1.5 standard deviations (e.g., around -1.05 or -1.25). This ensures a high-probability fill in the "sweet spot" while capturing the bulk of the move.

## TGIF (Thank God It's Friday) Retracements
- **Weekly Range Profile:** If the market has trended heavily in one direction from Sunday evening through Thursday (a one-sided weekly range), expect Friday to behave differently.
- **Friday Profit Taking:** Because smart money and institutions take profits ahead of the weekend (especially when geopolitical risk is high), Friday will often retrace 20% to 30% back into the weekly range.

## Trading Psychology & Chart Setup
- **Naked Charts vs. Annotated Charts:** While learning, annotate your charts with NDOGs, NWOGs, and liquidity pools. However, your ultimate goal is to trade a "naked chart" and hold these levels in your head or on a physical notepad. Staring at lines on a chart often causes traders to stubbornly impose their will on the market rather than reacting to live price action. 
- **Turtle Soup:** A reversal pattern named as a snub to the "Turtle Traders" (who bought breakouts). It involves letting retail traders buy a breakout above an old high, and then trading the inevitable algorithmic reversal back into the range (a stop hunt).
