# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 1 Summary (August 5, 2024)

## Core Mindset and Psychology
- **Don't Rush the Process:** Do not try to water down the concepts into a simple "1-2-3 pattern". Avoid the "TikTok mentality" of wanting to learn everything in a 5-minute video.
- **Embrace Adversity and Uncertainty:** As a new trader, you must be comfortable not knowing what the next candle will do. Trading is inviting risk; you are stepping into the jungle.
- **Avoid Funded Account Traps:** Don't rush into passing combines just to trade real money, as this introduces "scar tissue" and severe anxiety. 
- **The Micro Contract Rule:** Trade with **one micro-contract** and take only **one trade a day** initially. This physically prevents you from blowing an account and limits fear/greed.

## Algorithmic Price Delivery & Time
- **The Algorithm:** The market is not driven by buyers and sellers; it is controlled by an algorithm. 
- **Time is the First Hallmark:** Price is delivered on the basis of time. Certain specific times of the day are predisposed for certain setups.
- **Timezones:** All times referenced are **New York Local Time (Eastern Standard Time)**. You must adjust your charts to New York time.

### Key Pre-Market Windows (The 3 Opportunities)
The algorithm sets up the stage during specific 30-minute windows. These are your "Pre-Market Ranges":
1. **7:00 AM to 7:30 AM** (AM Session starts at 7:00 AM)
2. **8:00 AM to 8:30 AM**
3. **9:00 AM to 9:30 AM**
- *Note:* The true **Opening Range** is the first 30 minutes *after* the 9:30 AM bell (9:30 AM to 10:00 AM). It is a 30-minute opening range, not 15 minutes.

## The Draw on Liquidity (Where is Price Going?)
The algorithm reaches for two main things:
1. **Old Highs and Old Lows** (Liquidity / Stops)
2. **Inefficiencies** (Fair Value Gaps)

### Determining Bias: "Smoothness" vs "Jaggedness"
- **Smoothness (Still Waters):** The market will draw towards areas of "smoothness" to disrupt orders (stop-losses). Smoothness is defined by **Relative Equal Highs** or **Relative Equal Lows**. 
- **Jaggedness (Shark Attacks):** Areas where price has already been disrupted, spiked through, or chopped up (like shark teeth marks). Do not expect price to draw into areas that have already been heavily cannibalized.

### High-Probability Relative Equal Highs / Lows
- **High-Probability Relative Equal Highs (Priming):** When you have two swing highs, and the one on the **left** is slightly higher than the one on the **right**. This creates a trap that makes retail think it's strong resistance. The algorithm will likely sweep above this.
- **High-Probability Relative Equal Lows (Failure Swing):** When you have two swing lows, and the one on the left is slightly lower than the one on the **right** (a higher low). 

*Bias Rule:* Look at price *prior* to the Pre-Market Windows. Whichever side has the smooth relative equal highs/lows is your bias. If both sides have them, wait for the first one to be disrupted, then target the other.

## Core Timeframes
You only need three timeframes for these concepts:
- **15-Minute Chart** (Static, refer to it once in the morning)
- **5-Minute Chart** 
- **1-Minute Chart** (Where you watch execution and delivery)

## Technical PD Arrays (Premium/Discount Arrays) Defined
- **Displacement:** When the market runs aggressively against a pre-session, pre-day trend, or price swing direction.
- **Bullish Breaker:** When the market drops and takes out relative equal lows, every up-close candle in that drop is part of the breaker. 
  - *Key Detail:* The most sensitive part of the breaker is the **last up-close candle** before the drop. When price returns to it, look for the *bodies* of the candles to respect it, even if wicks pierce it.
- **Fair Value Gap (FVG):** An inefficiency in price delivery.
  - **SIBI (Sell-side Imbalance, Buy-side Inefficiency):** A fair value gap with a down-close candle.
  - **BISI (Buy-side Imbalance, Sell-side Inefficiency):** A fair value gap with an up-close candle.
- **Volume Imbalance:** A slight separation or gap between the bodies/wicks of consecutive candles.
- **Inversion Fair Value Gap:** An old Fair Value Gap that price trades completely through, which then acts as support/resistance in the opposite direction.
- **Consequent Encroachment (CE):** The midpoint (50% level) of an inefficiency/gap or a price leg.
- **New Week Opening Gap (NWOG):** The gap between Friday's close and Sunday's 6:00 PM EST open. It acts as a massive draw on liquidity.

## The Judith Swing
At 9:30 AM (during the opening range), the market will often create a **Judith Swing** — a fake move opposite to the real algorithmic draw on liquidity, designed to trap breakout traders before reversing to the true target (the smooth relative equal highs/lows).
