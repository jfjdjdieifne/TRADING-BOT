# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 12 Summary
**Date:** August 19, 2024 (Afternoon Review)

## 1. AMD (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution) and The Daily Range
*   **The Power of 3 (AMD):** In a bullish profile, the daily candlestick will open near its low and close near its high. 
*   **The Opening Price as a Barrier:** Many traditional traders (like Larry Williams in the 90s) struggled to buy below the opening price, preferring to buy strength. ICT solves this by identifying the 9:30 AM open and waiting for the "Judas Swing" (Manipulation)—a fake drop below the opening price to trap early sellers and sweep sell-side liquidity.
*   **Low-Risk Entry:** Buying at or below the 9:30 AM opening price during a bullish daily bias provides the lowest-risk entry for capturing the true daily trend (Distribution).

## 2. The Lunch Macro (11:30 AM to 1:30 PM)
*   **Purpose:** The lunch macro acts as a scheduled retracement mechanism against the morning session's trend. It algorithmically seeks the liquidity (stops) generated by the morning's profitable positions.
*   **Targeting the Retracement:** If the morning session was bullish (trending up), the algorithm will reverse at 11:30 AM to hunt sell stops.
*   **How to Find the Target:** To locate the exact sell-stop target the lunch macro will reach for, go backward from 11:30 AM and find the *first swing low that formed after 10:00 AM*. The algorithm will drop to sweep the liquidity resting beneath that specific low before resuming the daily trend.

## 3. Holding for Larger Moves (Time and Macros)
*   **Submitting to Time:** To capture large intraday runs (e.g., 100+ handles), you must submit to the daily timeframe's schedule. You cannot strangle the trade by trailing your stop loss too tightly during the lunch retracement.
*   **Afternoon Targets:** If you enter during the morning Judas Swing, you should expect to hold the position until at least the afternoon macros begin.
*   **The Final Macros:**
    *   **3:15 PM - 3:45 PM:** The first late-afternoon macro which begins to spool price toward the daily target.
    *   **3:45 PM - 4:00 PM (Market on Close):** The 15-minute MOC macro aggressively draws price into the final higher-timeframe liquidity pool (e.g., a daily FVG or daily buy-side liquidity).
    *   **4:01 PM - 4:15 PM:** During earnings season, an additional highly volatile algorithmic run occurs immediately after the regular session closes.

## 4. Higher Timeframe Alignment
*   **Top-Down Bias:** Intraday trades must align with the higher timeframe objectives (Daily/Weekly). If the Daily chart has unmitigated buy-side liquidity or an open volume imbalance above, all intraday bullish setups have a high probability of success, while intraday shorts should be taken with minimal leverage as they are counter-trend.
*   **Black Swan Exceptions:** The only factor that overrides the standard algorithmic delivery of price is massive geopolitical intervention (e.g., a sudden war or major conflict in the Middle East), which will force manual intervention and large, erratic down-close candles.
