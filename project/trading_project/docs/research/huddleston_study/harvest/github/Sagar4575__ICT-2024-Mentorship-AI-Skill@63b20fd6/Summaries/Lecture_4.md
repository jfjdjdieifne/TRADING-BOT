# ICT 2024 Mentorship - Lecture 4 Summary (August 8, 2024)

## The 8:30 AM EST Macro & Economic Calendar
- **Economic Calendar:** Always check the economic calendar (like forexfactory.com or econoday.com) before 8:30 AM EST. It acts as a "TV guide" to tell you when the market will inject extreme volatility.
- **8:30 Spooling:** Even if there is *no* high or medium-impact news at 8:30 AM, the algorithm will still begin to "spool" (rapidly reprice). A news event merely exaggerates the speed and range of the algorithmic run.
- **Algorithmic Repricing vs. Buying Pressure:** Massive, instantaneous moves (e.g., 100 handles in 1 second at 8:30 AM) are NOT organic retail buying/selling pressure. They are automated, algorithmic repricing events designed to trap retail traders who try to play the breakout.

## Determining Directional Bias Using Gap Clusters
At 8:30 AM EST, determine your directional bias by looking at the clustering of gaps:
- Locate the current price. 
- Look above and below price for the presence of New Day Opening Gaps (NDOGs) and New Week Opening Gaps (NWOGs) from the past 5 days/weeks.
- If there is a massive cluster of untested NDOGs and NWOGs *above* the current price, the algorithmic draw on liquidity is highly probable to push price *upward* into that cluster. 
- You use these time-based gaps to anticipate the direction *before* the 8:30 run happens. 

## Advanced PD Arrays: Breakers & FVGs
- **Bearish Breaker:** A high, a low, a higher high (that takes out buy-side liquidity), and then a strong displacement lower. 
- If a Fair Value Gap (FVG) forms *inside* the range of that breaker during the displacement lower, that FVG becomes an extremely high-probability area of resistance.
- **Reclaimed Fair Value Gaps:** If price trades completely through an FVG, it is not necessarily broken. If it returns and the *bodies* of the candles respect the gap while only the *wicks* push through it, the FVG has been "reclaimed" and is valid for entry.
- **Wicks vs. Bodies:** The wicks do the damage (grabbing liquidity and sweeping stops), but the bodies tell the true narrative.

## SMT Divergence (Optional Qualifier)
- **The Matrix:** ICT monitors the NASDAQ, S&P 500, and the Dow Jones simultaneously on a small timeframe (e.g., 1-minute to 5-minute).
- **SMT Divergence:** If two of the indices make a higher high, but the third fails to make a higher high, this is SMT Divergence. 
- It acts as a "crack in correlation" and is a strong secondary confirmation that a reversal or explosive move is imminent. It is not required for a trade, but acts as a high-probability qualifier.

## Psychology: The Danger of Premature Demo Trading
- **Boring Repetition:** Professional trading is extremely boring and redundant. Do not seek excitement from the charts. 
- **Tape Reading First:** Do not open a demo account right away. You must spend weeks purely *tape reading* (watching price move into your marked NDOGs/FVGs without executing a trade).
- **Hide the PnL:** Never look at your floating Profit and Loss (PnL) while in a trade. Managing a trade based on your equity curve is toxic; you must manage the trade based on what the price action is doing on the chart.
- **Track Your Physiology:** Notice if you start breathing quickly or breathing through your mouth when the market moves against you. Check this immediately, or it will escalate into panic when you trade real money.
